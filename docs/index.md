# Everyring

Everyring shows your Apple Health data as concentric progress rings, where **every ring has its own period**.
Close your steps ring today, your running ring this week and your workouts ring this month, or keep a rolling
"last 7 days" ring above its goal all the time.

Everyring is free, has no ads and no account, and all your data stays on your device.

## Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue: <https://github.com/usernamefourzerofour/Everyring/issues>

**A ring shows 0?** Make sure Everyring may read that data: open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then
Apps → Everyring, and turn on the data types your rings use.

**What is the difference between "resets" and "rolling"?** A ring that resets (today, this week, this month or a
custom period) fills up during the period and starts over when the next one begins. A rolling ring always shows
the last N days up to now: old days drop out as new ones begin, so the value can also go down.

**Which day does my week start on?** Your region's first day of the week. You can change it in Settings.

Everyring is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. It is not affiliated with Apple.
