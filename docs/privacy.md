# Everyring Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 15 November 2026*

Everyring is designed so that your data never leaves your devices.

## Health data

- Everyring **reads** the Apple Health data your rings use (for example steps, active energy, exercise minutes,
  distances, workouts, sleep, mindful minutes, time in daylight and water). You choose which types it may read, and
  you can change this at any time in the Health app.
- Everyring **never writes** to Apple Health.
- Health data is processed **only on your device**. It is never sent to the developer or any third party, never
  used for advertising or marketing, and never sold.

## Data stored by the app

Your ring sets, settings and a cache of your progress are stored locally on your device, in a container shared
only with Everyring's own widgets. If you use the Apple Watch app, your ring sets are sent from your iPhone to your
Apple Watch directly through Apple's Watch Connectivity; your watch reads its own Health data.

## No tracking

Everyring has no account, no server, no iCloud sync, no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not collect
any personal data.

## Deleting your data

Deleting Everyring removes all data it has stored. Your Health data stays in Apple Health.
